FAQ

Common questions about Lingo

Is Lingo free to use?

Yes! Lingo offers a free tier with 2 hours of translation per day. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited translation.

How does the first month free work?

When you upgrade to Lingo Pro, your first month is completely free. You can cancel anytime before the month ends.

What browsers are supported?

Lingo works on Chrome and Microsoft Edge. We're planning to add more browsers soon.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Protected fields like passwords and payment forms are never translated. Your text is not stored or logged.

Can I use Lingo for work?

Yes! Lingo Pro is perfect for professional use with unlimited translation and priority processing.

Is there an annual plan?

Yes! Annual billing is $19.99/year, which saves you compared to monthly billing.
